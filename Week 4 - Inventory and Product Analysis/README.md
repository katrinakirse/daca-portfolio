# Week 4 | Inventory and Product Statistics

## Overview

SQL analysis of product inventory, sales quantities, and category-level price statistics. Common table expressions and window functions are used to organize calculations and rank products within categories.

## Analysis included

- Compare units sold with units in stock by product category.
- Summarize product counts and retail-price ranges by category.
- Rank products by retail price within each category.
